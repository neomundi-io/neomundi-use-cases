# NeoMundi Use Cases

**One measurement primitive. Multiple applications. Multiple infrastructures.**

**NeoMundi brings the signal. Each external system retains interpretation, policy and action authority.**

This is the canonical public index of NeoMundi use cases and interoperability evidence. NeoMundi measures runtime behavior and provides an independent signal. Partners retain decisions, operational authority and execution mechanisms.

From an intuition, on September 27, 2025, to our first pilots and partners.

## Use cases

### 01 · AI Business / Express Test Purchase — Insurance and risk evidence

**Pilot in progress.** NeoMundi and Express Test Purchase independently observe the same execution. NeoMundi measures runtime behavior; Express Test Purchase cross-references permissions, actions, logs and receipts. Their records remain separate and can be matched for review. This is not a sequential processing chain. The pilot evaluates complementary evidence for an underwriter. Risk assessment belongs to the underwriter. The controlled audit below does not establish an insurance outcome.

No dedicated insurance evidence; [related controlled audit](https://github.com/neomundi-io/use-case-aibusiness-runtime-conformity).

### 02 · OmarAGI / REVAS — Agentic control and routing

**Pilot 01 documented · Pilot 02 live planned.** Pilot 01 documents the transformation of five synthetic NeoMundi runtime observability cases into REVAS pre-action admissibility artifacts. Pilot 02 is a planned live test with ControlTower; it is not presented as completed. This evidence is limited to the documented synthetic cases and establishes neither general reliability nor production validation.

[Partner repository](https://github.com/effacermonexistence/omaragi-neomundi-pilot).

### 03 · OASSE / FAM / Gatekeeper — Policy and enforcement

**Validated pilot.** NeoMundi measures. **OASSE policy layer applies deterministic rules**. Gatekeeper enforces the verdicts **ALLOW / TRANSFORM / HOLD / DENY**. Policy, decisions and enforcement remain external to NeoMundi. The documented pilot supports this separation within its tested scope, not general policy correctness.

[Repository](https://github.com/neomundi-io/use-case-oasse-runtime-governance).

### 04 · AI Business / Express Test Purchase — Controlled AI agent audit

**Validated pilot.** NeoMundi records runtime measurements while Express Test Purchase independently checks the same execution through the agent’s actions, authoritative log and receipt. Records remain separate and cross-referenceable; neither replaces the other. Behavioral measurement and receipt conformity are distinct variables. The business validator remains external to NeoMundi. Evidence concerns a synthetic reference environment, not general agent reliability or underwriting validation.

[Repository](https://github.com/neomundi-io/use-case-aibusiness-runtime-conformity).

### 05 · FactNotebook — Evidence reconstruction

**Validated pilot.** FactNotebook ingests NeoMundi measurements and their provenance to reconstruct evidence and governance objects. The PubMedQA experiment documents integration without altering provenance. Evidence reconstruction does not by itself establish factual truth or general medical validity.

[Repository](https://github.com/neomundi-io/use-case-factnotebook-pubmedqa).

### 06 · DriftShield / HIC — Parallel observability

**Validated minimum pilot.** NeoMundi and DriftShield/HIC independently observed one real execution. Observations remained distinct and cross-referenceable without sharing the raw response or changing the existing X/Y/Z authority. Evidence is limited to this single execution; it does not establish repeatability, production-scale performance or general interoperability. A dedicated public evidence package is not available here.

No dedicated public NeoMundi repository identified.

### 07 · SEILX — Governance through verifiable evidence

**Pilot in progress.** The pilot evaluates SEILX capture, preservation and time-anchoring of artifacts associated with a NeoMundi observation, so integrity and provenance can be checked later. No final result is claimed. Evidence integrity is distinct from the truth of the observed content and from operational permission.

No dedicated public NeoMundi repository identified.

### 08 · ManChine / RGE-01 / REB-01 / RiCo-01 — Admissibility and execution continuity

**Architecture documented · Validation in progress.** NeoMundi observes runtime conditions. RiCo-01 verifies continuity of the runtime governance correspondence and identifies any effect on the portability of prior governance. RGE-01 produces a new **Admissibility Determination** when a new decision point exists. REB-01 applies the applicable governance state at the consequential execution boundary.

The linked repository is an architectural reference. Full machine-to-machine validation is not yet available. RiCo does not produce the Admissibility Determination; observation grants no execution authority to NeoMundi.

[Repository](https://github.com/neomundi-io/use-case-rico-observation-to-admissibility).

### 09 · Kabbalah App / RAG Test Bench — RAG runtime quality detection

**Validated field test.** A production-oriented bilingual RAG evaluation instrumented 60 generations per reported round. Alerts fell from **45/60** before prompt correction to **1/60** after correction; average stability rose from **0.68–0.77** to **0.915–0.923**.

> In this RAG test bench, the NeoMundi runtime signal detected a real degradation before its technical cause was identified, then reflected the return to more stable behavior after correction.

NeoMundi did not identify the best model. An evaluation component belonged to a model family included in the comparison; response length also confounded generation ranking. Long prompts and latency imposed integration constraints. These observations are specific to this test bench and are not a general accuracy guarantee.

[Evidence and report](./evidence/kabbalah-app-rag/README.md).

### 10 · VERITAS — Signed signal verification

**Adapter validated · Live interoperability pending.** The documented state is a merged NeoMundi RGC v0.2 adapter, green post-merge validation and a tested synthetic verification path in VERITAS. The target is independent verification of a real signed NeoMundi observation, followed by VERITAS governance evaluation and an auditable receipt or Evidence Packet. Complete live interoperability has not yet been demonstrated. Signature verification, governance evaluation and execution permission remain separate responsibilities.

[Repository](https://github.com/veritasfuji-japan/veritas_os).

## Maturity and evidence

These labels qualify a specific scope; they are not certifications or a universal progression scale. Public evidence may lag behind a documented pilot status.

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Architecture documented | Responsibilities and interfaces are described; this alone does not demonstrate a live integration. |
| Pilot in progress | Evaluation is underway; no final result is established. |
| Controlled run completed | A bounded run was completed under specified conditions; no general or production-scale validation follows. |
| Validated minimum pilot | A minimal interaction was demonstrated; for DriftShield/HIC, one real execution only. |
| Validated pilot | The stated pilot objective was demonstrated within its documented scope; partner technology is not generally validated. |
| Live interoperability pending | A complete exchange with a real observation remains to be demonstrated, even if an adapter or synthetic path works. |
| Validated field test | A field evaluation documents a bounded observation; here, RAG degradation and recovery after correction. |
| Adapter validated | Adapter checks and a synthetic verification path are documented; this does not replace live interoperability evidence. |

## What this proves

Within the documented scopes, the same measurement primitive can support separate orchestration, policy, evidence, observability and diagnostic functions. The linked artifacts distinguish completed tests, architectural references and evaluations still in progress. Measurement can remain independent of the systems that interpret and act on it.

## What this does not prove

These cases do not establish general certification, factual truth, regulatory compliance, insurability, production-scale reliability or superiority of a model or partner. A signal or signed observation is not an authorization. Missing public artifacts are not replaced by status labels, and synthetic or single-execution tests do not establish general live interoperability.
